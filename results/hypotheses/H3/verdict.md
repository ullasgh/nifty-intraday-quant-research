Module: +1.21 bps, t = 1.04, 1,866 sessions (recon: 1,867). Per-year figures land between
the recon's `10:00` and `10:01` rows without a consistent bias toward either -- 2018 and 2021
sit nearer `10:01`, 2024 nearer `10:00`, 2025 outside both by ~1 bp. A systematic one-minute
label shift (plausible given CLAUDE.md rule 4: the bar labelled 10:00 covers [10:00, 10:01))
would move every year the same way, and it does not. The scatter is consistent with sampling
noise on an effect this small. The verdict is identical under all three recon rows, so this
does not change the conclusion.

The failure mode the spec warned about -- a result near zero or off by orders of magnitude,
as when H2's `horizon=1` measured one minute of a 373-bar session -- did not occur.

## Why this was built after the answer was known

Reconnaissance was already decisive. The module exists for the audit trail: a verdict nobody
can reproduce is not a verdict. The mandatory checkpoint-panel reduction (two rows per
session, both resolved BY TIME LABEL) is the H2 regression guard and is covered by a
realistic-session fixture that fails if the reduction is removed.

## Conclusion

Fails on all four counts the hypothesis could fail on: magnitude ~14x short of the gate,
t = 1.04, sign unstable across years, and the sign is POSITIVE -- weak momentum, the direct
opposite of the hypothesised reversal. Latency-insensitive, which here only confirms there is
nothing present to decay.

Read together with H2 (cross-sectional reversal in the OVERNIGHT gap: -24.30 bps, real but
illiquid-concentrated and decayed), the pair says cross-sectional reversal on Nifty-100 is
specific to the overnight gap and absent from intraday moves. That fits the liquidity-provision
mechanism: overnight is where order imbalance accumulates with no continuous trading to absorb
it; intraday, continuous liquidity clears imbalance as it arrives, leaving nothing to revert.

**Consequence: further reversal variants on price-derived intraday signals are low-value.**
